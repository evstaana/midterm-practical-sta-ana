Why we clean the bay

Every rainy season, plastic from streets and waterways washes up on the bay's edge. Bottles, sachets, and bags tangle in the mangroves and break down into pieces small enough for fish to eat.

Baybay Cleanup is a student-run volunteer drive. Everything we collect is sorted, weighed, and handed to the city's recycling partners, and the totals are shared with everyone who joined.

What to bring

Wear

Closed shoes you can get wet, a hat, and light clothes that cover your arms.

Bring

A refillable water bottle, sunscreen, and a small towel. We'll have water refill stations.

Leave at home

Single-use plastic bottles and anything valuable you don't want to carry on the sand.
